# Delivery windows — Harbour Basket (synthetic policy)

**This document is fictional demo data, not a real retailer's policy.**

## Metro Melbourne delivery

- Delivery is available seven days a week in metro Melbourne postcodes, generally between **7:00 am and 9:00 pm**.
- Customers choose a **two-hour window** at checkout.
- Orders placed after **9:00 pm** are eligible for the next day's windows, subject to capacity.

## Fees and thresholds

- Delivery is **free on orders of $50 or more**. Below $50, a **$9.00** delivery fee applies.
- Click and collect is always free. Collect from the nominated store in the chosen window; bring the order number.

## After you order

- You can change the window or cancel without charge **up to 2 hours before** the window starts.
- Inside that cut-off, a cancellation may be refused if the order is already being picked, or a restocking fee may apply (demo: $5).
- If we miss the window by more than 30 minutes, the delivery fee is refunded (or a $9 credit is issued if delivery was free).

## Unattended delivery

- Perishable items will only be left unattended if you have authorised it in the order notes and there is a safe, shaded place (for example a side gate).
- Otherwise the driver will attempt a courtesy call and may return the order to the store for later collection.
