# Returns — Harbour Basket (synthetic policy)

**This document is fictional demo data, not a real retailer's policy.**

## Standard grocery returns

- Unopened, non-perishable pantry, household, and personal-care items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery or click-and-collect, with a receipt or the order number.
- Fresh produce, chilled dairy, meat, seafood, bakery, and deli items are **not returnable for change of mind**. If they arrived damaged, unsafe, or not as described, see Refunds.
- Opened items are only accepted if they are faulty or the wrong product was packed.

## How to start a return

1. Have your order number ready (for example, ORD-5001).
2. Tell the shopping assistant, contact support, or bring the item to a store customer service desk.
3. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Store credit can be offered if you prefer.

## Exclusions

- Infant formula may only be returned if unopened and within the dated use-by window, or if the product is faulty.
- Alcohol is not sold in this demo catalogue.
- Sale or clearance items follow the same food-safety rules as full-price items.
