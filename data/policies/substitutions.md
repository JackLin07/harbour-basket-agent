# Substitutions — Harbour Basket (synthetic policy)

**This document is fictional demo data, not a real retailer's policy.**

When an ordered item is out of stock, we may offer a substitute depending on the preference saved on the order:

- **allow-similar** — We pack a close match of similar size and quality, usually within **10% of the original price**. If the substitute is more expensive, you are charged the original price. If it is cheaper, you are charged the lower price.
- **contact-me** — We will not pack a substitute until you approve one. If we cannot reach you before the delivery window, the item is refunded.
- **refund** — We never substitute. The missing item is refunded automatically.

## Dietary and allergen rules

- We will **not** substitute a gluten-free, vegan, vegetarian, halal, or allergen-free item with a product that does not meet the same restriction.
- If the customer profile lists allergens to avoid (for example peanuts or tree nuts), substitutes must not contain those allergens.
- Baby formula is never auto-substituted; we always refund or contact the customer.

## Out of stock at pick

If no acceptable substitute exists, the line is refunded and noted on the invoice.
