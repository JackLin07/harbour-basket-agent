# Refunds — Harbour Basket (synthetic policy)

**This document is fictional demo data, not a real retailer's policy.**

## Missing, damaged, or incorrect items

- Report a missing item, damaged goods, or a packing error within **48 hours** of delivery (or collection).
- We will refund the line, send a replacement, or do both if you want the product and a goodwill gesture is appropriate.
- A photo helps for damaged goods but is not required for a clearly missing item that appears on the invoice.

## Quality issues

- If fresh food is unsafe or well below expected quality (for example spoiled milk, off seafood, or mouldy bread), we refund the item and may add a small gesture of goodwill.
- We may ask which batch or use-by date was on the pack so we can check the rest of the load.

## How refunds are paid

- Refunds go back to the **original payment method** and typically appear within **3 business days**.
- If that is not possible, we issue store credit to the customer account.
- Delivery fees are only refunded when the whole order is cancelled in time, the order never arrives, or we miss the window (see Delivery).

## Cancellations

- Cancel more than 2 hours before the window for a **full refund**, including the delivery fee.
- Later cancellations are handled case by case if picking has already started.
