# Frequently asked questions — Harbour Basket (synthetic)

**This document is fictional demo data, not a real retailer's policy.**

## Can the assistant place a paid order?

In this demo, the assistant can search the catalogue, build a basket, look up an existing order, and log an issue. It cannot take payment or finalise a live checkout.

## What if something is out of stock?

The catalogue shows live stock for this demo. If stock is zero, the assistant should not add the item to the cart. It can suggest a similar product and explain the substitution policy.

## How do I check an order?

Provide your order number (for example ORD-5002) or ask about your most recent order. The assistant will only show orders that belong to the signed-in customer.

## I have a food allergy.

Tell the assistant which allergens to avoid. Product records include allergen and dietary tags. The assistant should not recommend items that contain those allergens, and should not invent allergen information if a field is missing — it should say it does not know.

## Can I talk to a human?

Yes. Ask the assistant to log an issue or to escalate, and a support teammate will follow up. This is a demo: the log is stored in memory and in the issues dataset.

## Pricing and currency

All prices are in Australian dollars (AUD) and are **synthetic**. They are not real supermarket prices.
