# Synthetic data notice

Everything under `data/` is **fictional**. It was written for a portfolio demo of
a grocery shopping assistant.

- It is **not** real supermarket data, pricing, stock, or customer information.
- Product names, brands, and prices were invented. They are not copied from any
  retailer's catalogue.
- Customer names, addresses, emails, and orders are made up.
- Store policies describe a fictional banner called **Harbour Basket**. They are
  not the policies of Coles or any other real retailer.
- The repository name refers to the role this project was prepared for; the
  dataset itself is unaffiliated with that company.

Do not present these prices, policies, or products as real.
